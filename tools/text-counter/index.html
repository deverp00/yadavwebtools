How these counts are defined

Characters are counted with or without spaces, depending on the toggle. The no-spaces figure removes every whitespace character, which is what platforms like Twitter and SMS limits typically use. Words are counted by splitting on any run of whitespace, so hyphenated or slashed phrases count as a single word.

Sentences are detected by punctuation marks (. ! ?) and by paragraph breaks. Paragraphs are separated by one or more blank lines. Reading time assumes an average speed of 200 words per minute; speaking time uses 130 words per minute. Both figures round up to the nearest second.

Keyword density ignores common stop words (the, a, and, of, etc.) when the toggle is on, and shows the ten most frequent words along with their percentage share of the total. Useful for SEO audits, headline checks, and quick readability reviews.
