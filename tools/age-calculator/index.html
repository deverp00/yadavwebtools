How the age is calculated

The calculator first finds the difference in calendar years, months, and days between your date of birth and the reference date (today, or the date you choose). If the current day-of-month is smaller than your birth day, one month is borrowed and the days of the previous month are added — the same method used by official age-certification forms.

Totals such as weeks, days, and hours are computed from the exact millisecond difference, so they remain accurate across leap years and month-length changes. The next-birthday countdown is calculated from today's date and correctly rolls over to the following year once the birthday has passed.
