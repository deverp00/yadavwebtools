Privacy Policy

What data we collect, what we don't, and why.

Last updated: 15 January 2026 Effective: 15 January 2026

Yadav Web Tools is built around a simple principle: your data stays on your device. This policy explains exactly what that means, what limited information our hosting provider sees, and what third-party services are involved when you use specific tools.

1. Data you enter into tools

Every tool on this site runs entirely in your browser. Text you type, files you upload, images you resize, PDFs you merge, and JSON you edit are processed locally using standard browser APIs. None of it is sent to us or to any server we control. When you close the tab, that data is gone from our side because it was never on our side to begin with.

2. What our hosting provider sees

This site is served by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub's infrastructure logs standard request information — IP address, user agent, requested URL, timestamp — to operate the service and protect against abuse. This is outside our control and is covered by GitHub's own privacy policy. We do not have access to these logs.

3. No analytics, no trackers, no ads

We do not run any analytics service, tracking pixel, session recorder, or advertising network. There are no cookies set by this site. No fingerprinting scripts are loaded. Nothing about your visit is profiled or sold.

4. Local storage

A small amount of data is stored in your browser's localStorage so the site works smoothly between visits:

  • Theme preference — whether you chose light or dark mode.
  • Forex rate cache — a copy of the previous day's exchange rates, used only to show a percentage change on the forex dashboard.

This data stays on your device and can be cleared at any time from your browser settings. It is not transmitted to us.

5. Third-party services

A few tools rely on external services for data you can't compute offline. When you use those specific tools, your browser makes a direct request to the service listed. Those services will see your IP address and the query you sent, just as if you visited their site directly.

  • Currency Converter — open.er-api.com for exchange rates, api.coingecko.com for crypto prices, api.frankfurter.app for historical charts.
  • Forex Dashboard — TradingView for candlestick charts; open.er-api.com for the price cards.
  • Hosting Checker — dns.google for DNS lookups; ipwho.is for IP geolocation.
  • Fonts — Google Fonts serves the Inter typeface used across the site.
  • Libraries — cdnjs.cloudflare.com and unpkg.com deliver PDF, ZIP, and QR libraries.

All other tools make no external requests at all.

6. Microphone and speech

The Accessibility Tool can transcribe speech using your browser's Web Speech API. When you press the mic button, your browser may send audio to Google's speech recognition service (in Chrome) or process it locally (in some other browsers). This behaviour is controlled by your browser and operating system, not by us. We never receive, store, or transmit the audio.

7. Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. Since no personal information is collected from any user, there is nothing to delete on request.

8. Your rights

Because we do not collect personal data, there is nothing for us to export, correct, or delete. You retain full control of anything stored locally by clearing your browser's site data at any time.

9. Security

The site is served over HTTPS. Client-side tools use standard browser APIs that do not introduce additional attack surface. We do not accept uploads or store files, so there is no server-side database to breach.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a material way, the "last updated" date at the top will change and the new version will be posted here. Continued use of the site after a change constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to hello@yadavwebtools.com.
